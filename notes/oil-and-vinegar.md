# Vinaigrette Pairing Model
*Flavored oils + flavored vinegars — Dad's Cookbook reference*

Built on the five pillars (salt, fat, acid, sweet, umami). Used two ways:
- **Store:** name one bottle → get dark, white, and wildcard partners.
- **Make:** name a pair → get ratio, salt, pepper, herbs, add-ins, and uses.

---

## 1. Core Rule: Bridge or Contrast

- **Bridge** — both bottles share a note (citrus + citrus, herb + herb). Safe, cohesive.
- **Contrast** — one supplies what the other lacks (savory heat + sweet fruit). Memorable.
- **Intensity match** — bold oil needs a bold vinegar; delicate oil needs a bright, light one.

---

## 2. Families

### Oils
| Family | Examples |
|---|---|
| Herb | basil, rosemary, Tuscan herb, herbes de Provence, oregano |
| Allium | garlic, roasted onion, scallion, wild mushroom & sage, black garlic |
| Citrus | lemon, Meyer lemon, lime, blood orange, orange |
| Heat/Smoke | chipotle, jalapeño, harissa, Calabrian, garlic chili, wood-smoked |
| Rich/Nutty | butter, walnut, toasted sesame, almond |
| Plain EVOO | mild/buttery (Arbequina) or robust/peppery (Koroneiki) |

### Vinegars
| Family | Examples | Sweetness |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Fruit | fig, cherry, blackberry, raspberry, pomegranate, blueberry, strawberry | High |
| White Fruit | peach, mango, pineapple, apricot, pear, apple, cranberry pear | Med-High |
| Citrus White | Sicilian lemon, grapefruit, key lime, tangerine | Medium |
| Savory | traditional, Neapolitan herb, oregano white, garlic | Medium |
| Sweet-Heat | jalapeño, honey-ginger, serrano honey | Med-High |
| Dessert | espresso, chocolate, vanilla, maple | High |
| Plain | red wine, sherry, rice, cider, champagne | Low |

## 3. Pairing Matrix

| Oil | Bridge | Contrast | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Herb | Savory, Citrus White | Dark Fruit (strawberry, fig, raspberry) | Dessert |
| Allium | Savory | Dark Fruit (fig, pomegranate), White Fruit (peach, pineapple) | Vanilla, chocolate |
| Citrus | Citrus White | Dark Fruit (blueberry, pomegranate), White Fruit (mango, pineapple) | Neapolitan herb |
| Heat/Smoke | Sweet-Heat | Dark Fruit (blackberry, raspberry, cherry), White Fruit (mango, pineapple), espresso | Citrus White alone |
| Rich/Nutty | Dessert (maple) | Dark Fruit (cherry, raspberry), White Fruit (apple, pear) | Savory herb |
| Plain EVOO | Anything | — | — |

**Wildcard:** traditional balsamic works with every oil.

## 5. Ratios: Sweetness Sets the Oil

| Vinegar | Oil : Vinegar | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | 3 : 1 | House rule |
| White balsamic | 2 : 1 | Lighter sugar |
| Dark fruit / dessert | 2 : 1 to 3 : 2 | Syrupy = less oil |
| Mixed (plain + flavored) | 3 : 1 | Splits sweetness and bite |

**Base batch:** ¼ cup total dressing, enough for 4 side salads.

---

## 6. The Finish (Five Pillars)

**Salt** — Dissolve in vinegar first; salt won't dissolve in oil. Start ¼ tsp kosher per ¼ cup dressing. Taste on a leaf.

**Pepper**
- Black: herb, allium, savory pairs
- Pink peppercorn: fruit pairs
- Skip: heat oils

**Herbs by oil family**
| Oil family | Herbs |
|---|---|
| Herb | basil, oregano, flat-leaf parsley |
| Allium | thyme, chives |
| Citrus | mint, dill, chives |
| Heat/Smoke | cilantro, scallion |
| Rich/Nutty | sage, tarragon |

**Umami** (savory pairs only) — ½ tsp Dijon or 1 Tbsp grated parm. Dijon also stabilizes the emulsion.

**Sweet** — Honey only with plain vinegars. Fruit balsamics are already sweet.

**Aromatic** — 1 Tbsp minced shallot macerated in the vinegar 10–15 min removes harsh bite.

---

## 7. Build Order

1. Shallot (optional) + vinegar → rest 10 min
2. Salt → stir to dissolve
3. Dijon / honey if using
4. Oil → shake in a jar
5. Pepper + herbs
6. Taste on a leaf → adjust salt first, then acid

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Too sharp | Pinch more salt before more oil |
| Flat | Salt, then a few drops of plain vinegar |
| Cloying | Splash red wine or cider vinegar |
| Separates fast | ½ tsp Dijon, shake hard |
| Oil solidified (fridge) | 10 min at room temp |

---

## 9. Match to the Greens

| Greens | Best pair style |
|---|---|
| Arugula, chicory, radicchio | Bold oil + dark fruit |
| Spinach | Citrus or allium + dark fruit |
| Butter lettuce, spring mix | Citrus or herb + white fruit |
| Romaine | Herb or allium + savory |
| Slaw, cucumber | Heat or sesame + sweet-heat or white fruit |
| Tomato + mozzarella | Basil or Tuscan herb + traditional or strawberry |

---

## Notes for the Kids
Start with salt in the vinegar, then oil. Taste on a leaf, not a spoon. If it's too sharp, try salt before adding more oil — salt tames acid better than you'd think.
